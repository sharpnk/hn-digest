## 9. [A Necessary History of the Oddest Letter: W](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49778195) (↑69, 💬42)
**Article:** Danny Bate traces the letter W from the fall of Rome, when Germanic-speaking rulers in formerly Latin lands needed to spell the sound /w/ that Latin no longer represented. **Comments:** Commenters focus on W's awkward English name, "double u," which makes acronyms like WWW and WWF longer to say than their expansions, and propose alternatives such as "dub," "dubya," "wee," or German "vay."
