## 2. [Trusting-Trust Attack against an Entire Linux Distribution](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49575515) (↑80, 💬15)
**Article:** This paper extends Ken Thompson's "trusting trust" attack beyond compilers by building a working version around GNU strip, an ordinary utility that manipulates finished ELF binaries rather than source code. In a real NixOS bootstrap, a single tampered strip binary propagates the backdoor through successive generations of strip and into the final standard environment, enabling malicious behavior across nearly all built binaries.

**Comments:** Several commenters noted the attack is not conceptually new, since Thompson himself said any program-handling tool would suffice, though they credited the paper as a concrete worked example. Others discussed David Wheeler's diverse double-compiling method as a potential defense, pointed out that current x86 bootstrap seeds are only 181 bytes so the attack mainly targets non-x86 platforms, and summarized it as malware on a build machine infecting its artifacts; a brief tangent about AI enabling script kiddies drew pushback.
