## 9. [RIP, vector database](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49923466) (↑238, 💬63)
**Article:** Turbopuffer announces v3, a storage architecture change that moves away from an ANN vector-primary index and makes ANN "just another" secondary index. The post traces v1 and v2, explaining that the vector-primary layout causes storage amplification, write amplification, and limited vectorization for non-vector query plans like text/regex search, filtering, aggregations, and SQL. **Comments:** Commenters compare the shift to Postgres vs. MySQL/InnoDB index design, noting MySQL secondary indexes point to stable primary keys while Postgres uses row TIDs, trading read indirection for less write amplification. Some also complain about page-load problems, "AI slop," or the term vector database, though turbopuffer's founders are defended as hand-writing the post.
