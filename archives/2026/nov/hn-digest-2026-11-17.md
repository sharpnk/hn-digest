## 4. [350 years ago, Ole Rømer discovered that light has a finite speed](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50536813) (↑152, 💬45)
**Article:** The article recounts Ole Rømer’s 1676 prediction that Io would emerge from Jupiter’s shadow about ten minutes later than expected, demonstrating that light has a finite speed. By comparing eclipse timing as Earth moved toward and away from Jupiter, he estimated light took about 22 minutes to cross Earth’s orbital diameter, roughly 225,000 km/s—about 25% low but impressive for the time. The finding was gradually accepted and later confirmed by Huygens, Newton, and Bradley’s stellar aberration work.

**Comments:** Commenters share comics and videos explaining the measurement, then dig into clock accuracy, eclipse timing, and the one-way speed of light and synchronization conventions. A geometry brain teaser about the North Pole draws discussion, and some criticize the article for labeling Arabic scientists by religion while calling Roger Bacon “English.” Others note pop-up annoyances, a Douglas Adams quote, and concerns about AI-generated content.
