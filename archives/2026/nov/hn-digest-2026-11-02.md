## 1. [Astra can read and write weights to improve Qwen](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50341174) (↑1308, 💬412)
**Article:** MIT CSAIL researchers describe “Weight-Space Programming,” where the frontier LLM Astra directly reads and writes neural-network weights without gradient descent. Astra identifies what small networks were trained to do, edits failing policies, and improves Qwen3.5-0.8B on MATH-500 from 7.9% to 48.1% by editing 0.016% of weights. The authors frame this as an early step toward LLM-driven weight optimization and interpretability.

**Comments:** Commenters are broadly shocked, with debate over whether the ability is emergent, trained for recursive self-improvement, or explainable by prior weight-space and mech-interp work. Skeptics note the Qwen gain mainly removes a looping failure rather than adding new capability, while others worry about safety and RSI implications. Several also ask whether the technique generalizes to larger models, specialized subnetworks, or replacing optimizers like Adam.
