## 4. [Stealing Reasoning Traces from Proprietary LLM APIs](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49257876) (↑423, 💬168)
**Article:** A research paper ("Stolen Thoughts") demonstrates that encrypted chain-of-thought blocks returned by Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google APIs can be replayed into weaker sibling models; jailbreaking the weaker model recovers the stronger model's hidden reasoning in plaintext. Using this technique on 6,708 public agent trajectories, the researchers reconstructed 315,320 reasoning blocks and found 704 privacy artifacts including API keys, passwords, and emails — 64 of which appeared only in the hidden reasoning. All three providers acknowledged the report and subsequently fixed the attack.

**Comments:** Commenters disputed the word "stealing," arguing users paid for those tokens and that model outputs trained on collective human knowledge shouldn't be proprietary; one framed it as "liberating." Others discussed the distillation implications, noting this likely explains how Chinese labs catch up to frontier models. Several expressed surprise that providers reused encryption keys across models and sessions, while one commenter noted the practical fix is to bind encryption to specific model/session. A prior researcher confirmed he'd tried similar replay attacks without success and praised the authors for taking it all the way. Some expressed fatigue with opaque reasoning tokens, saying they're willing to cede visibility to providers to focus on their business.
